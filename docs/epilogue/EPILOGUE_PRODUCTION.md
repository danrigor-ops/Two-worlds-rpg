# Epilogue Production: The First Train of Tomorrow

> **Status: WORKING PROPOSAL (28 November 2026).** This expands the handover lines "railway callback, character epilogues" and "ending with the duo boarding another train".

## 1. Flow
1. After the Chapter 10 screen, a train made of morning light waits at the Heart of the Crossing (FC-02). Its sign says BANDAR SENJA.
2. **EP-01, Bandar Senja Station, morning, 1963.** A new line opens today. This mirrors the prologue's last train with a first train.
   - The humans can't see the spirits, but they all agree the station feels crowded this morning, in a nice way.
3. **Character epilogues.** Talk to each friend on the platform. The train waits until you've talked to everyone.
   - **Elias** starts a radio programme, "Things We Remembered Today", for spirits and humans.
   - **Maris** reports that Hutan Seribu Bunga is blooming again, and plants flowers at the station.
   - **Taro** is the carnival's new, "mostly honest" Ringmaster's assistant. He sends a paper bird ahead of the train.
   - **Galen** goes back to his gate and leaves it open: "the good kind of quiet".
   - **The Keeper**, in a clean blue coat, hands out free crossing tickets. He is an Oathkeeper again, "the kind that catches people".
   - Overheard humans: the stationmaster has goosebumps, and the girl from Chapter 8 says thank you for the door in the moon.
4. **The duo board another train.** "Where does this train actually go?" "Somewhere neither of us has been." "PERFECT." Then `epilogue_completed` and the **THE END** screen, which returns to the title.
